💡 Pro Tips for Getting Paid

⏰
Send reminders at the right time Tuesday to Thursday mornings (10am–12pm) have the highest email open rates. Avoid Mondays and Fridays.
🔗
Always include a payment link Add your PayPal, Wise, Payoneer or bank details in every email. Make it as easy as possible to pay you.
📎
Attach the original invoice Clients sometimes claim they never received it. Attach the PDF every single time — no excuses.
📅
Always give a clear deadline Instead of "as soon as possible," write "by Friday, [date]." Specific dates create urgency and accountability.
📋
Use written contracts next time A simple contract with payment terms (e.g. net 14 days) protects you and reduces late payments by 60%.
